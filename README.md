# SuperRsk: All in One QoL Addon (Auto Equip, Fast Loot, XP Bar) for WoW Forever

**An all in one quality of life addon for World of Warcraft: Forever**, made by SuperRsk and friends.
Every module can be switched on or off on its own. Type `/sg` in game to open the menu.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/GAMEBOOK/SuperRsk/releases/latest)**

## Modules

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Auto Equip** | Spots gear upgrades for your class and spec (armor type, weapon DPS, hit and crit ratings) and offers to equip them. Shows the upgrade % in item tooltips. Picks the best quest reward. |
| **Auto Fishing** | One key casts and hooks. Takes your best pole and fishing gear out of your bags, applies lures, gives your weapons back when attacked. A card counts catches, value and time, with a Put my gear back on button. |
| **Quest Item Cleanup** | `/sg menage` lists the quest items you no longer need (certain with Questie) and throws them away in one click. |
| **XP Bar** | A clean, movable XP bar with the XP of your finished and ongoing quests, rested XP, XP per hour. |
| **Auto Loot** | Instant fast loot, loot list, sound when bags are full. |
| **Junk** | Bags full while looting: offers to throw away the grey item worth the least, with its vendor and auction house price. Never without your click. |
| **Merchant** | Sells your grey items and repairs your gear at any merchant, no click needed (guild money optional). |
| **Gear Durability** | A small widget warns you when your gear drops below 20 % (threshold, style and size to taste). |
| **Auto Spell Ranks** | Replaces old spell ranks on your action bars with the new rank when you learn it. |
| **Auto Shot / Wand** | Auto shot and wand timer: tells you when to stop moving so the shot is not lost. |
| **Flight Points** | Every flight point of your faction on the world map, known ones marked. |
| **Raid Markers** | Puts your raid marker and your friends' markers with one key. |
| **Swap Layer in group** | Multiboxing with the LayerSwap addon. Left button: Leave group when grouped, otherwise Swap layer. Right button: Invite team members (list in the tooltip), they accept on their own. |
| **Spy Ping** | With the Spy addon: click an enemy in the list to target and ping them for your group. Better enemy detection. |
| **Honorable Kills** | Small movable widget with your honorable kills. Hover it for the guild top, click it for the full guild ranking. |
| **Quick Interact** | The game targets the nearest thing you can use, with an icon above it: ores, herbs, chests, quest objects, fishing bobbers, everything (up to 20 m). One press of your Interact key takes it. |
| **Boost FPS** | One click graphics settings for more FPS and a sharper picture. Your previous settings come back when you turn it off. |
| **Weather and Fog** | Removes rain, snow, sandstorms and fog. |
| **Tsquid Area** | Lightsaber sound on critical hits (off by default). |

## Install

1. Download `SuperRsk-x.y.z.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/GAMEBOOK/SuperRsk/releases/latest).
2. Unzip it into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\` (you get a `SuperRsk` folder).
3. Restart the game, then type `/sg`.

## Optional addons

Two modules need another addon, SuperRsk tells you in game when one is missing:

- **Spy Ping** needs **Spy**.
- **Swap Layer in group** needs **LayerSwap**.

## Commands

- `/sg` or `/srsk`: menu
- `/sg move`: move every element of every module (also the Move button at the top of the menu)
- `/sg spe`: talent tree used by Auto Equip
- `/sg tir`: measured auto shot / wand timings

# SuperRsk Toolbox (français)

**La boîte à outils maison de SuperRsk et ses potes pour WoW Forever.** Chaque module s'allume ou se coupe à part. Tape `/sg` en jeu pour le menu.

Auto Equip (meilleur équipement pour ta classe et ta spécialisation), ramassage instantané, barre d'XP avec l'XP des quêtes, minuteur de tir auto et de baguette, mise à jour des rangs de sorts, points de vol sur la carte, interaction rapide (filons, plantes, coffres, objets de quête, pêche, tout ce qui s'utilise), marqueurs de raid, swap de couche en multibox avec LayerSwap, ping des ennemis Spy, classement des victoires honorables de la guilde, boost FPS, sans météo ni brouillard.

**Installation** : télécharge le zip de la [dernière version](https://github.com/GAMEBOOK/SuperRsk/releases/latest), dézippe-le dans `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\`, relance le jeu, tape `/sg`.
